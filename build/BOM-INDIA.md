## Sourcing guide

- **Pipe/plumbing market:** A1–A4 threaded BSP fittings (SS304), copper washers — bring the shopping list and test-fit threads.
- **Steel market:** A5 (Ø30×1.5 tube), A6 (2″ pipe offcut), G1–G2 (MS plate/rails).
- **Refractory/IndiaMART:** furnace cement, kaowool, sodium silicate.
- **Electronics (Robu.in / Robocraze / Amazon.in):** HV module, load cell + HX711, Arduino, thermocouple.
- **Drone/RC shops:** NGK CM-6 (Fortune India ₹1,699; generics ₹400–800).
- **Gas agency/burner shop:** hose, adapter, valves.
- **3D printing:** your H2D — ABS/ASA (see `HYBRID-TEST-GUIDE.md` §3 for settings).

## Open items — verify before buying

1. **Thread standard:** confirm BSP (G) fittings; some import shops sell NPT — don't mix.
2. **Plug reach:** with boss + bushing stacked, check the CM-6 tip protrudes ~2–3 mm into the chamber and doesn't foul anything.
3. **Coupler fit test:** print one C1 and one C2 first; adjust +0.5 % X/Y if ABS/ASA shrinkage makes them tight.
4. **Filament pick:** ABS (~₹710) vs ASA (~₹1,950) — ASA prints the tall cones more easily.
5. **Cylinder policy:** confirm campus rules for LPG testing (outdoors, long hose).

## Appendix — later upgrade to full metal (delta only)

When you're done iterating, the printed cones/intake can be replaced by the welded metal version: SS304 sheet 2 mm (~₹350) for both cones + TIG weld service (₹500–1,500) + ER308L filler (~₹300), per `cad/CAD-NOTES.md`. Everything else in this BOM (A, C–I) carries over — the metal stack re-threads onto the welded body unchanged.
